Gem Arbogast and I cleaned the appliance's internals, combatting a food safety disaster on par with Sizzle & Crunch. On wiping the asscrack of the fridge, Arbogast described the experience thusly:

**"Ummm... 0 out of 10. The more cherry we cleaned up, the more there was. It just kept getting stickier."**

The Herculean deed of disposing of the fruit's slimy guts evokes comparisons to the lesser, but similarly annoying task of slaying the hydra.

**"I didn't know my fridge had an asscrack,"** Arbogast continued, **"I could have gone my whole life not knowing my fridge had an asscrack."**

Whoever is responsible for this atrocity will be caught and duely prosecuted. I cannot help but consider the possibility of a mole within our ranks.
